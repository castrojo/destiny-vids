# H-15 — Deliver the campaign: assemble the Open Gaming Collective cut end to end

**What:** every other issue in this plan builds a capability. This one is the
deliverable #11 actually asked for — the multi-episode Halo: Combat Evolved
campaign starring the Open Gaming Collective, assembled, scored, HUD'd and
reviewed. Without it the plan ends with a working toolchain and no movie.

**Scope:**
- Author the campaign file against the draft map in
  [`../design.md`](../design.md#12-proposed-episode-map-draft): six episodes over
  the ten CE missions, each alternating dialogue movements with combat movements.
  The draft is a hypothesis written before the corpus existed — **rule 2 says the
  outline changes when the shots disagree**, so expect to rewrite beats, and
  record what changed and why.
- Run the whole chain per episode: roster → campaign → story → render → HUD →
  intro deck → plates.
- Publish the miss report, not just the cut. Unmatched beats are the honest
  output of an index this size and are what tells the owner where the corpus is
  thin.
- Review each episode against the three rules before it ships: no unclean source
  laundered by the HUD; no beat filled by widening the pool; no `character` tag
  or callout naming someone who is not visibly in that shot.
- Record the outcome in the plan directory — final episode map, track
  assignments, shortfalls, and any beat the footage vetoed.

**Acceptance:**
- [ ] Six episodes render, each alternating dialogue and combat movements with
      one continuous track per combat movement.
- [ ] The closing episode ends on the Bazzite CTA beat.
- [ ] Every credited person appears in the shots they are credited on.
- [ ] The miss report is published alongside the cut.
- [ ] No footage, music or logo asset is committed to the repo.

**Depends on:** H-07 (a corpus), H-10 (scored assembly), H-11 (the HUD),
H-12 (the cast), H-14 (the intro deck)

**Automatable:** partly — the chain runs itself; the editorial review of six
episodes, and every judgement about whether a beat reads, is human work.
