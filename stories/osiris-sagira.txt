# Osiris and Sagira, Curse of Osiris opening cinematic.
#
# The anonymous-Guardian beats are dealt out across the story rather than run
# off at the top. Every ensemble anchor in the source sits in the opening
# firefight, so ordering them by source time credits the whole month inside the
# first twelve seconds and then goes silent. Spread this way, each of the five
# Guardian shots — the crouch-run, the Guardian suspended in void light, the
# courtyard pillars, the Vex spire, the fall through crossing beams — can carry
# a contributor plate at the cadence in tools/plate.py. See "Spread the
# credits" in README.md before reordering; moving a beat moves a credit.
#
# The last firefight beat runs under the approach to the gateway on purpose:
# the war is still going when Osiris arrives, and the calm gateway ends it.
three helmeted guardians move in a low crouch-run through a teal-lit ruin corridor
two guardians push forward side by side, one in heavy plate armor aiming a rifle
a brass vex minotaur looms with a single glowing red eye, charging a violet weapon
extreme close-up on osiris's masked face beneath an ornate gold headdress
a hooded guardian leaps into the air with void energy blazing around a raised fist
a lone robed figure climbs a frost-dusted stairwell, seen from behind and dwarfed
osiris reaches into a floating cluster of glowing cyan glyphs, gold headdress lit
a sprawling firefight spans a shattered vex courtyard, guardians perched on broken pillars
osiris stands his ground as a towering vex war-frame's claw reaches toward him
sagira's spiked gold ghost shell hangs alone in near-total darkness
extreme overhead angle on osiris swinging a blazing solar sword onto a vex machine
osiris dives through the air wreathed in solar flame and burning wings
seen from behind, osiris vaults over broken stonework toward a spread of vex machines
a hooded guardian braces atop a jagged black vex spire and fires a rifle through embers
face-clear close-up on osiris's weathered eyes above his gold chevron mask
osiris and sagira approach a massive glowing triangular gateway, small and distant
guardians leap and fall through crossing beams in a chaotic firefight of machines
a long calm view of the triangular gateway atop sunlit ruined steps
