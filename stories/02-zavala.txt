# 02-zavala — the Zavala cut. Sequence position 02 (see docs/cuts/02-zavala.md).
#
# ONE CINEMATIC, SKIPPED FORWARD. Every beat below is cut from
# yt_destiny_2_the_final_shape_launch_trailer and sits at or after the previous
# one on that cinematic's own timeline, so the cut only ever skips forward:
#
#   python3 tools/story.py stories/02-zavala.txt --dir segments \
#       --from-video yt_destiny_2_the_final_shape_launch_trailer --forward-only
#
# The beat order IS the timeline. There is no sequencer and no second
# cinematic; the five movements below are the skip-forward points, and each one
# names the source timecode it resumes at.
#
# A HERO CUT FOR ZAVALA. The camera serves him: every movement is built to
# arrive on one of his six clean shots, and the last of them — the ghost of a
# smile at 1:25 — is the one the whole cut is walking toward. The antagonist
# gets one beat, wide and brief, face never given. There is no per-enemy
# framing anywhere in here, by design.
#
# THE FICTION BENT TO THE FOOTAGE. Zavala has eight clean shots in the whole
# index and six of them are in this cinematic; the other two are in the
# Lightfall launch trailer, which is a different source and therefore a
# different cut. Nothing here was widened to unclean footage and no shot was
# invented. What the outline could not reach — including the owner's 1:50
# reveal point — is recorded in docs/cuts/02-zavala.md and corpus/zavala.json.
